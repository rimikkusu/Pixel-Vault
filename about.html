ABOUT PIXEL VAULT

A student-made corner of the internet for the games, artwork, and ideas that shaped how we play.

WHAT IS PIXEL VAULT?

THE IDEA

Pixel Vault is a small digital archive of classic games. Each cover, release year, and platform is an invitation to remember where a favorite genre began or discover something new.

It is a front-end school project built with HTML and CSS. The handmade logo, frames, lines, and arrows helped shape the site from the start.

WHY THIS THEME?

Games combine art, music, storytelling, and technology. A pixel-art world felt like the right setting for a collection that celebrates all four.

  • Explore memorable games across several systems.
  • See original covers beside project pixel artwork.
  • Keep a simple, readable record of gaming history.

GAMING GLOSSARY

FPS
First-person shooter: the world is seen through the character's eyes.
RPG
Role-playing game: characters grow through a story and its choices.
NPC
Non-player character: a character controlled by the game.
Platformer
A game focused on movement and navigating levels.
